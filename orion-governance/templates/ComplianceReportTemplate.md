to downplay them.

detail that the change is auditable without re-deriving it.

## 5. Recommendation

(Approve / Approve with Conditions / Reject, or the equivalent for a
non-review report), with conditions stated as testable, closeable items.

dropped) -- per `ConfigurationIntegrityStandard.md`.
